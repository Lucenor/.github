# Security policy

This policy covers every Lucenor repository that does not have its own `SECURITY.md`.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report a vulnerability privately by email to **[security@augur.engineering](mailto:security@augur.engineering)**. Do not open a public issue, pull request or discussion for it.

Include what you can of:

- the affected repository, product or URL, and the version or commit;
- what an attacker could do with it;
- steps to reproduce it, or a proof of concept;
- how to credit you, if you want credit.

## Scope

In scope:

- the code in Lucenor's repositories;
- the services and sites Lucenor runs from them.

Out of scope:

- vulnerabilities in third-party dependencies with no Lucenor-specific impact; report those to the upstream project;
- denial of service by traffic volume, spam, and social engineering of Lucenor staff;
- automated scanner output with no demonstrated impact.

## What to expect

- We acknowledge a report within 3 business days.
- We send an initial assessment within 10 business days, and updates until the issue is resolved.
- We ask that you keep the details private until a fix is released, and we coordinate the disclosure date with you.

We do not take legal action against anyone who reports a vulnerability in good faith under this policy, avoids privacy violations and service disruption, and gives us reasonable time to fix it before disclosing it.
